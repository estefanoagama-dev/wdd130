Ecuador

Located on South America's west coast, Ecuador is a vibrant nation named after the equator that runs through it. It features a rich mix of indigenous and modern traditions, historic colonial cities like Quito and Cuenca, and four breathtaking geographical regions: the Coast, the Andes, the Amazon, and the Galápagos Islands.
