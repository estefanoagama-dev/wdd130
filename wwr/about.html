LESFO Whitewater Adventures

Our mission is to provide unforgettable rafting experiences through adventure, teamwork, and respect for nature. We are committed to safety, fun, and creating lasting memories. Our motto: "Ride the Rapids, Live the Adventure!"

History

LESFO Whitewater Adventures was created with a passion for nature, adventure, and exploring Ecuador's beautiful rivers. Inspired by the amazing landscapes of the country, our company was founded to share exciting rafting experiences with people from around the world. Today, we continue to promote teamwork, safety, and unforgettable adventures.
